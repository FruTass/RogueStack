# **Dragon Slayer**
***Race**: Haseldan*

> Refer to [[Before Progression|before progression]] to get very helpful late-game passive and active skills.

### 1) Pit Fighter 
[*Spear Base Class* ](https://rogue-lineage.fandom.com/wiki/Pit_Fighter)

You can purchase a **spear** at [[Map#**Oresfall**|Oresfall]], always buy the bronze to maximize XP gain (XP gain is per every hit, not per damage dealt)
- *Refer to the [[Map]] for getting around areas of interest.*

> Pit Fighter is the base spear class that specializes in long range and doing long damage combos.

**Trainer Location**: [[Map#**Oresfall**|Oresfall]], to the back where there's a huge pit arena, right next to where you buy the spears.


- Requirements:
	- 8 Zombie Scrooms ([[Map#**Sleeping Forest**|Sleeping Forest]])
		- 2 Zombie Scrooms per skill
- Payment: 
	- Each skill is 45 Silver
	- 180 Silver total
- Skills:
	- Active:
		- **Serpent Strike**: Delivers a swift stab that temporarily [[Core Mechanics#**Poison**|poisons]] an opponent.
			- *The poison effect bypasses blocking and mana shields.*
		- **Tripe Strike**: Unleashes three rapid strikes and knocks an opponent back on the final hit.
	- Passive:
		- **Dash Mastery**: Replaces rolls with long, swift side dashes.
		- **Plate Training**: Allows you to wear heavy armour without slowing you.
			- This ability is shared with the Warrior (Sword) base class.

### 2) Dragon Knight
[*Orderly Spear Super Class*](https://rogue-lineage.fandom.com/wiki/Dragon_Knight)

> Dragon Knights use the powers of a dragon to deal heavy hitting attacks with their small move set. Dragon Knights can use dragon-like abilities to stun enemies or empower themselves.

**Trainer Location**: [[Map#**Flowerlight Town**|Flowerlight Town]], leaning against the merchant building with a 

- Requirements:
	- Max Pit Fighter
	- 8 Zombie Scrooms per skill, 24 total
	- Gain 15 [[Core Mechanics#**Orderly** The good|Orderly]] 
		- Drink 5 [[Core Mechanics#**Orderly** The good|Tespian Elixirs]] before any quest | In [[Map#**Oresfall**|Oresfall]]: Sick Girl Quest, Homeless Man Quest | In [[Map#**Alana**|Alana]]: Turnip Quest | In [[Map#**Flowerlight Town**|Flowerlight]]: Toilet Hand Quest | In [[Map#**Southern Sanctuary**|Southern Sanctuary]]: Scroom Guy Quest
			- Refer to the [[Map]] for getting around areas of interest.
- Payment:
	- Each skill is 100 Silver + 1 Acorn Light
		- *You obtain Acorn Lights by travelling to [[Map#**Sunken Passage**|Sunken Passage]], going inside and to the right, and straight once you enter the 'staircase'. They're the glowing ones dangling by the ceiling.*
	- 300 + 3 Acorn Lights total
- Skills:
	- Active:
		- **Spear Crusher**: Channel your inner wyvern and stab forwards, dealing 40 damage. 
			- 8-9 second cooldown. 
			- Breaks mana shield.
		- **Dragon Roar**: Bellow like a dragon, stunning nearby players. 15 Second Cooldown.
			- Can be used while climbing
			- Disables mana charge for 2 seconds.
			- Spear skills can be stacked during Dragon Roar.
		- **Dragon Blood**: Slowly drains health while active, increases physical damage by 20%, attack speed, move speed by 3, reduces all damage taken by 10%.
			- Can be used without a spear
			- Automatically turns off at low health.

***When you max Dragon Knight, buy yourself a Mythril Spear, you won't need to get more experience.***
