# **Seri: Ronin**
***Race**: Ashiin*

> Refer to [before progression](CoreMechanics/BeforeProgression.md) to get very helpful late-game passive and active skills. 

---

### 1) Warrior
[*Sword Base Class*](https://rogue-lineage.fandom.com/wiki/Warrior)

> Swords are very versatile weapons, as they possess moderate range, speed, and damage. This means that it may be easier to adapt to a situation that involves fighting against varying weapons.

You can purchase a **sword** at [Oresfall](CoreMechanics/Map.md#oresfall), [Sentinel](CoreMechanics/Map.md#sentinel), or [Alana](CoreMechanics/Map.md#alana) always buy the bronze to maximize XP gain (XP gain is per every hit, not per damage dealt)
- *Refer to the [Map](CoreMechanics/Map.md) for getting around areas of interest.*

**Trainer Location:** The back of sea of dust, if you see a little tree stump 'bridge' you're going the right way. He's in a wooden house, to the right as soon as you enter.

- Requirements:
	- 6 ZScrooms 
- Payment:
	- 35 Silver per skill
	- 105 Silver total (because you spawn with Mercenary Carry)
- Skills:
	- Active:
		- **Pommel Strike**: Deliver a swift blow with your sword's hilt, damaging and [concussing](CoreMechanics/CoreMechanics.md#concussion) your opponent.
		- **Action Surge**: A green aura envelops you, allowing you to unleash a flurry of slashes for a brief moment.
	- Passive:
		- **Mercenary Carry**: Empowers your body with newfound strength, allowing you to run and dash whilst carrying somebody.
			- *This ability is shared with the Ashiin race.*
		- **Plate Training**: Fortifies your body with new training, allowing you to wear heavy armour without slowing your step.
			- *This ability is shared with the Pit Fighter base class.*
