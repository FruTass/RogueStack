# **Don: Deepknight**
***Race**: Scroom*

> Refer to [before progression](CoreMechanics/BeforeProgression.md) to get very helpful late-game passive and active skills. 

---
### 1) Pit Fighter
[*Spear Base Class* ](https://rogue-lineage.fandom.com/wiki/Pit_Fighter)

> Pit Fighter is the base spear class that specializes in long range and doing long damage combos.


You can purchase a **spear** at [Oresfall](CoreMechanics/Map.md#oresfall), always buy the bronze to maximize XP gain (XP gain is per every hit, not per damage dealt)
- *Refer to the [Map](CoreMechanics/Map.md) for getting around areas of interest.*

**Trainer Location**: [Oresfall](CoreMechanics/Map.md#oresfall), to the back where there's a huge pit arena, right next to where you buy the spears.

- Requirements:
	- 8 Zombie Scrooms ([Sleeping Forest](CoreMechanics/Map.md#sleeping-forest))
		- 2 Zombie Scrooms per skill
- Payment: 
	- Each skill is 45 Silver
	- 180 Silver total
- Skills:
	- Active:
		- **Serpent Strike**: Delivers a swift stab that temporarily [poisons](CoreMechanics/CoreMechanics.md#poison) an opponent.
			- *The poison effect bypasses blocking and mana shields.*
		- **Tripe Strike**: Unleashes three rapid strikes and knocks an opponent back on the final hit.
	- Passive:
		- **Dash Mastery**: Replaces rolls with long, swift side dashes.
		- **Plate Training**: Allows you to wear heavy armour without slowing you.
			- *This ability is shared with the Warrior (Sword) base class.*

### 2) Church Knight
[*Chaotic Spear Super Class*](https://rogue-lineage.fandom.com/wiki/Church_Knight)

> Great damage output, decent health pool and incredible versatility makes this class one of the best Super classes.

**Trainer Location**: Yeti Cave in Tundra

- Requirements:
	- Max Pit Fighter
	- Obtain 50 [Chaotic](CoreMechanics/CoreMechanics.md#chaotic-the-evil)
		- 25 Grips Or 5 Assassinations
	- 15 Zombie Scrooms
- Payment:
	- 150 Per Skill
	- 450 For Everything
- Skills: 
	- Active:
		- **Impale**: Applies a [bleeding](CoreMechanics/CoreMechanics.md#bleeding) status effect, unique to this class, it stops all health gain for about 10 seconds.
			- *Additionally applies the Laceration injury.*
		- **Light Piercer**: Fires 3 projectiles that stun and deal Moderate damage.
			- *Goes through physical block but does not cancel it. Completely bypasses and breaks Mana shields.*
	- Passives:
		- **Church Knight Helmet**: Gain the Church Knight Helmet, which has more health than both the 'bucket' helmet, and the Sigil Helmet. You never lose this.
	- Class Traits: (Obtained with first skill)
		- **Prince's Gift**: Grants the Church Knight the Church Knight's Outfit & the Sanctum Spear
			- The Sanctum Spear features a unique M1 and M2. It is soulbound and cannot be disarmed. Speak to the Sanctum Spear again to receive another spear if it is lost
				- Soulbound means that you don't lose the object, even on death. (Only when you wipe do you lose it)
			- The Church Knight's Outfit provides 1.2x Health and 1.2x regen.
			- The Church Knight's Helmet provides 1.3x Health, identical to the Sigil Helmet.
