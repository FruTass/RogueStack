Must-have before class progression:

Immediately after spawn:
1) Buy Assassin's Armor at Sentinel so you can outrun people trying to grip you (25 Silver)

Mercenary Carry:
- Normally, when you carry someone that's knocked (Pressing V) you can't run or dash with them on your shoulder, this Sword Base Class passive let's you do that and it's essential late game.

1) Buy a **bronze** sword from either Sentinel, Alana, or Oresfall
	- Head to the Zombie Scrooms (ZScrooms) in Sleeping Forest
	- Kill 7 Zombie Scrooms
	- Make your way to Sea of Dust, to an unmarked wooden house past the ravine, and past the tower, **you don't have to climb anything to get there.**
	- Speak to the Warrior trainer inside the house.
		- Each skill is 35 Silver
		- Pray you get a passive and not an active skill, otherwise you'll keep having to spend money; test on a knocked player to see if you got it.


Spear Dash
- When you press Q (Dash) to the sides, you'll normally roll. If you want a better 'roll', get this Spear Base Class passive. Most Ultras are running this dash unless they have a niche reason not to.

1) Buy a **bronze** spear from Oresfall
	- Head to the Zombie Scrooms (ZScrooms) in Sleeping Forest
	- Kill 8 Zombie Scrooms
	- Make your way back to Oresfall, all the way to the end and to the left, go around the huge pit arena, and go down the stairs. The trainer is there.
	- Speak to the Pit Fighter Trainer.
		- Each skill is 45 Silver
		- When you receive a passive skill, dash sideways to see if you got it.

Scholar Class Maxed
- When you obtain Tomeless, you'll be casting spells left and right. The Mage Base Class, Scholar, gives you three passives that are essential for that spellcasting.

1) After you have a day, and you've unlocked mana, find the Tome Giver that's sitting down adjacent to one of the buildings, he's kind of tucked away, so look around. 
	- **If you're already Tomeless, ignore this step.**
