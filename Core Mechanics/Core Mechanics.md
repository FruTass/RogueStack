### Alignment:

Your alignment does not determine whether or not you can grip people, it's only a prerequisite for classes.
#### **Orderly**: The good
**Ways of attaining orderly:**

- Tespian Elixir (Potion)
	- Potion that when drank, does damage to you, but gives orderly. If it heals you instead, it's not giving more orderly.
		- *Tespians give no more orderly after you ALREADY have a select amount of it. It's important that before you do any quest, you drink your 5 Tespians, and THEN complete quests, otherwise you'll have to get more orderly later on.*
	- Craft recipe:
		- 2 Moss Plants (located primarily in [[Map#**Alana**|Alana]])
		- 1 Lava Flower (located in [[Map#**Sea of Dust**|Sea of Dust]])
		- 1 Scroom (located in [[Map#**Scroomville**|Scroomville]] or [[Map#**Renova Town**|Renova]])

- Jailing: 
	- Bounty Officers in [[Map#**Renova Town**|Renova]], [[Map#**Sentinel**|Sentinel]], and [[Map#**Oresfall**|Oresfall]] will tell you the location of chaotic players. Bring them back to the Bounty Officer to put them in jail and gain +1 orderly. If you prevent them from trying to exit the jail cell, after two minutes, their character will die (Rot), and you gain +3 orderly, for a total of +4 orderly.

- **Orderly Quests:**
	- Repeatable (Every in-game day):
		- Homeless Man (Orderly)
			- Located in [[Map#**Oresfall**|Oresfall]], behind the merchant, requires 1 Silver
		- Ice Protection Potion Quest (Orderly)
			- Located in [[Map#**Oresfall**|Oresfall]], all the way to the end, the NPC leaning on the wall, requires you to brew a Ice Protection Potion (1 Trote + 2 Snowshroom )
		- Turnip Quest (Orderly)
			- Located in [[Map#**Alana**|Alana]], an NPC near a table outside the houses, requires you to bring a Turnip from [[Map#**Flowerlight Town**|Flowerlight]], in [[Map#**Sleeping Forest**|Sleeping Forest]].
	- One-Time:
		- Sick Girl (Orderly / Chaotic)
			- Located in [[Map#**Oresfall**|Oresfall]], the first accessible house on the left, and up the stairs. Requires you to brew a Health Potion (1 Lava Flower + 2 Scrooms)
		- Scroom Quest (Orderly)
			- Located in [[Map#**Southern Sanctuary**|Southern Sanctuary]], an NPC near a table that you have to click through the dialogue for completion.
		- Toilet Hand Quest (Orderly / Chaotic)
			- Located in [[Map#**Flowerlight Town**|Flowerlight]], in one of the houses to the back. You need to speak to the hand in the toilet on the second floor, and then receive a love letter from the girl in the first floor, and give it back.

### Status Effects:

#### **Poison**: 
Poison is a status effect that will drain the player's health by 10% on proc. Poison will do this for a set period of time.
-  *Poison cannot kill a player (only knocking them down) and also cancels healing.*
