Must-have before class progression:

Immediately after spawn:
1) Buy Assassin's Armor at Sentinel so you can outrun people trying to grip you (25 Silver)

**Mercenary Carry**:
- Normally, when you carry someone that's knocked (Pressing V) you can't run or dash with them on your shoulder, this Sword Base Class passive let's you do that and it's essential late game.

1) Buy a **bronze** sword from either Sentinel, Alana, or Oresfall
	- Head to the Zombie Scrooms (ZScrooms) in Sleeping Forest
	- Kill 7 Zombie Scrooms
	- Make your way to Sea of Dust, to an unmarked wooden house past the ravine, and past the tower, **you don't have to climb anything to get there.**
	- Speak to the Warrior trainer inside the house.
		- Each skill is 35 Silver
		- Pray you get a passive and not an active skill, otherwise you'll keep having to spend money; test on a knocked player to see if you got it.


**Spear Dash**:
- When you press Q (Dash) to the sides, you'll normally roll. If you want a better 'roll', get this Spear Base Class passive. Most Ultras are running this dash unless they have a niche reason not to.

1) Buy a **bronze** spear from Oresfall
	- Head to the Zombie Scrooms (ZScrooms) in Sleeping Forest
	- Kill 8 Zombie Scrooms
	- Make your way back to Oresfall, all the way to the end and to the left, go around the huge pit arena, and go down the stairs. The trainer is there.
	- Speak to the Pit Fighter Trainer.
		- Each skill is 45 Silver
		- When you receive a passive skill, dash sideways to see if you got it.

**Scholar Class Maxed**:
- When you obtain Tomeless, you'll be casting spells left and right. The Mage Base Class, Scholar, gives you three passives that are essential for spellcasting.

1) After you have a day, and you've unlocked mana, find the Tome Giver that's sitting down adjacent to one of the buildings, he's kind of tucked away, so look around. You need to pay him 10 Silver.
	1) ONCE you've done this, you can purchase a weapon, which will override your tome, but it will let you buy the Scholar skills. If you come across a scroll you can pop it to gain the spell once you're Tomeless.
		- **If you're already Tomeless, ignore this step.**
2) After you have a tome, find [Fallion](https://rogue-lineage.fandom.com/wiki/Fallion) in one of his three spawns. Refer to the wiki to see where he spawns.  
3) It costs 70 Silver per skill, so 210 for all skills.

After you've done this, or while you're getting Fallion's training, get the right weapon for your class to progress.

If you're going a Chaotic class, you MUST obtain these before getting any chaotic alignment. If the Ultra you're going is Orderly, you can do this any time along your journey. 

- Drink a bare minimum of THREE Tespian Elixirs. This will be enough for both Sigil Knight Helmet and Tomeless.

Sigil Knight Helmet
- Everybody and their mother has the Sigil Knight Helmet. It offers around 10 or 15 more health than the regular 'bucket' helmet. If you see anyone with a 'bucket' helmet, assume they're a perma-freshie and they're awful at the game. 

1) You need to travel to Tundra.
	1) Find a howler, and obtain a howler fang from their drop.
	2) Go to Castle Sanctuary and give the howler to the man straight in front of the Orderly Barrier.
	3) Talk to the girl on the side to receive her "Promise".
	4) Deliver the Flower Promise to the sword that's near the Dragon Sage tower.
	5) Return to the quest girl to receive the Sigil Knight Helmet.

Tundra is very dangerous. The cold gives you frostbite and eventually kills you, and there's very few places to find warmth. [Refer to this guide](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mqdFlh-0ab0) if you want to try your luck at it, but assume you're most likely going to die, if not to the cold, then to howlers, without any help.

[Tomeless](https://rogue-lineage.fandom.com/wiki/Tomeless#Orderly)
- Tomeless is the foundation for every class's ability to extend combos and do extra damage, as well as snap certain spells, and block spells from literally everyone else with Tomeless with Mana Shield. If anything else, you need Tomeless guaranteed. 

1) Gain 3 days
2) Be Orderly
3) Sacrifice four unique races in the Tomeless room
4) Walk inside and dash sideways towards the blue orb, and touch it
	1) [Guide](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hSAQawqsnAk)
