
### Alignment:

Your alignment does not determine whether or not you can grip people, it's only a prerequisite for classes.
#### Orderly: The good
**Ways of attaining orderly:**

- Tespian Elixir (Potion)
	- Potion that when drank, does damage to you, but gives orderly. If it heals you instead, it's not giving more orderly.
		- *Tespians give no more orderly after you ALREADY have a select amount of it. It's important that before you do any quest, you drink your 5 Tespians, and THEN complete quests, otherwise you'll have to get more orderly later on.*
	- Craft recipe:
		- 2 Moss Plants (located primarily in [Alana](Map.md#alana))
		- 1 Lava Flower (located in [Sea of Dust](Map.md#sea-of-dust))
		- 1 Scroom (located in [Scroomville](Map.md#scroomville) or [Renova](Map.md#renova-town))

- Jailing: 
	- Bounty Officers in [Renova](Map.md#renova-town), [Sentinel](Map.md#sentinel), and [Oresfall](Map.md#oresfall) will tell you the location of chaotic players. Bring them back to the Bounty Officer to put them in jail and gain +1 orderly. If you prevent them from trying to exit the jail cell, after two minutes, their character will die (Rot), and you gain +3 orderly, for a total of +4 orderly.

- **Orderly Quests:**
	- Repeatable (Every in-game day):
		- Homeless Man (Orderly)
			- Located in [Oresfall](Map.md#oresfall), behind the merchant, requires 1 Silver
		- Ice Protection Potion Quest (Orderly)
			- Located in [Oresfall](Map.md#oresfall), all the way to the end, the NPC leaning on the wall, requires you to brew a Ice Protection Potion (1 Trote + 2 Snowshroom )
		- Turnip Quest (Orderly)
			- Located in [Alana](Map.md#alana), an NPC near a table outside the houses, requires you to bring a Turnip from [Flowerlight](Map.md#flowerlight-town), in [Sleeping Forest](Map.md#sleeping-forest).
	- One-Time:
		- Sick Girl (Orderly / Chaotic)
			- Located in [Oresfall](Map.md#oresfall), the first accessible house on the left, and up the stairs. Requires you to brew a Health Potion (1 Lava Flower + 2 Scrooms)
		- Scroom Quest (Orderly)
			- Located in [Southern Sanctuary](Map.md#southern-sanctuary), an NPC near a table that you have to click through the dialogue for completion.
		- Toilet Hand Quest (Orderly / Chaotic)
			- Located in [Flowerlight](Map.md#flowerlight-town), in one of the houses to the back. You need to speak to the hand in the toilet on the second floor, and then receive a love letter from the girl in the first floor, and give it back.

### Injuries:

#### Laceration
Slash Injury can be obtained from the Church Knight's Impale, Nocere, Sword injury, or a Monster. When a player has this injury, their max health is reduced by 20%.

---
### Status Effects:

#### Poison 
Poison is a status effect that will drain the player's health by 10% on proc. Poison will do this for a set period of time.
-  *Poison cannot kill a player (only knocking them down) and also cancels healing.*
---
#### Bleeding
Bleeding is a status effect applied from Church Knight's Impale and Abomination's Aura of Despair. 
- *This removes all forms of gaining health for a set amount of time, preventing natural regeneration, Health Potions, and external healing like Mederi.*
